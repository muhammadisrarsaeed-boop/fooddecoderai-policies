❓ Frequently Asked Questions

Scanning & AI

How many free scans do I get per day?

Free users get 10 AI food scans per day. The limit resets every 24 hours based on your local timezone. Upgrade to Pro (300/month) or Unlimited for more scans.

Why did the AI identify my food incorrectly?

AI food recognition is not perfect — complex, regional, or plated dishes can be misidentified. For best results, ensure good lighting, hold the camera steady, and photograph the food clearly. If recognition is wrong, use the manual text input option to describe the dish yourself.

Can I trust the nutritional information in recipes?

Nutritional data in Food Decoder AI is AI-estimated and should be used as a rough guide only. It is not suitable for medical diets, clinical nutrition plans, or managing health conditions. Always consult a registered dietitian or healthcare professional for accurate dietary guidance.

Can I use the app offline?

An internet connection is required to scan food and generate recipes (AI processing requires a live API connection). However, previously generated recipes saved to your history and favourites can be viewed offline.

Are my food photos stored anywhere?

No. Photos you take or upload are sent to Google's Gemini AI for real-time processing only. They are not stored on our servers after the analysis is complete.

How do I cancel my subscription?

Open Google Play Store → Tap your profile icon → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions → Find Food Decoder AI → Cancel subscription. You keep access until the end of the current billing period.

I was charged but my premium features are not unlocked.

This is usually a sync issue. Try: (1) Force-close and reopen the app, (2) Check your internet connection, (3) Go to Settings and sign out then sign back in. If the issue persists, email us at muhammadisrarsaeed@gmail.com with your Google Play order number.

How do I get a refund?

Within 48 hours of purchase: request directly through Google Play → Profile → Order history → Find order → Request refund. After 48 hours: email us at muhammadisrarsaeed@gmail.com with your order details and reason.

My subscription renewed but I forgot to cancel. Can I get a refund?

We understand this happens. Email us at muhammadisrarsaeed@gmail.com within 7 days of the unexpected renewal with your Google Play order number. We evaluate these on a case-by-case basis.

Account & Data

How do I delete my account and data?

In the app: Settings → Delete Account. This permanently deletes all your data from our servers. For full instructions, see our Data Deletion page. If you can't access the app, email us at muhammadisrarsaeed@gmail.com.

I forgot my password. How do I reset it?

On the login screen, tap "Forgot Password?" and enter your email address. A password reset link will be sent to your email. If you signed up with Google, use the Google Sign-In button instead.

Can I use the app on multiple devices?

Yes. Sign in with the same account on multiple Android devices. Your saved favourites and scan history sync automatically across devices via Firebase cloud sync.

Why does the app need camera and microphone permissions?

Camera is used for food scanning (the core feature). Microphone is used for the Voice Chef feature, which lets you speak to the AI while cooking. Both permissions are optional — you can use gallery upload or manual text entry without camera access, and Voice Chef can be ignored if you don't use it.

App Features

What languages does the app support?

Food Decoder AI supports 10 languages for recipe output: English, Arabic, French, Spanish, Urdu, Chinese (Simplified), Hindi, German, Portuguese, and Japanese. You can change your language in the app's onboarding screen or Settings → Preferences.

What is Voice Chef?

Voice Chef is an AI cooking assistant you can speak to while cooking. Ask questions like "How long should I cook this?" or "What can I substitute for butter?" — it understands your current recipe context and responds conversationally.

What is the Fridge/Pantry feature?

The Fridge and Pantry features let you log what ingredients you have available. The app can then suggest recipes using your available ingredients, helping reduce food waste.
