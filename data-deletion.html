Your Rights

Account & Data Deletion

You have the right to permanently delete your account and all associated data from Food Decoder AI at any time.

Last Updated: May 22, 2025
🗑️

We respect your right to be forgotten

Deleting your account permanently removes all your personal data from our servers. This action is irreversible.

📱 Method 1: Delete Account In-App (Recommended)

The fastest way to delete your account and all data is directly within the app:

  1. Open Food Decoder AI

    Launch the app on your Android device and make sure you are logged in to the account you want to delete.

  2. Go to Settings

    Tap the Settings icon in the bottom navigation bar (gear icon).

  3. Scroll to Delete Account

    Scroll to the bottom of the Settings page and tap the red "Delete Account" button.

  4. Confirm Deletion

    A confirmation dialog will appear. Read the warning carefully and tap "Delete" to confirm. This action cannot be undone.

  5. Done — Your Data is Deleted

    Your account and all associated data will be immediately deleted from our servers. You will be returned to the login screen.

📧 Method 2: Request Deletion by Email

If you cannot access the app (e.g., you lost your device or forgot your credentials), you can request deletion by email:

  1. Send an email to us

    Email us at muhammadisrarsaeed@gmail.com with the subject line: "Data Deletion Request"

  2. Include your account information

    In your email, include: your registered email address, the name you used when signing up, and a brief note confirming you want to permanently delete your account.

  3. We will verify and process your request

    We will respond within 72 hours to confirm your identity and initiate the deletion process.

  4. Deletion confirmed

    We will send a confirmation email once your account and all associated data have been deleted, within 30 days of receiving your request.

💳 Cancel Your Subscription Before Deleting

Deleting your account does not automatically cancel your Google Play subscription. You must cancel it separately to avoid future charges:

  1. Open Google Play Store

    Tap your profile icon in the top right corner of Google Play Store.

  2. Go to Payments & Subscriptions

    Tap Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions.

  3. Find Food Decoder AI

    Tap on the Food Decoder AI subscription.

  4. Cancel the Subscription

    Tap Cancel subscription and follow the confirmation steps. You will keep access to premium features until the end of the current billing period.

Recommended order: (1) Cancel subscription in Google Play → (2) Delete account in the app. This ensures no unexpected charges after account deletion.

ℹ️ What Is Not Deleted

  • Google Play purchase records: Google maintains records of transactions made through Google Play for billing and legal purposes. This is governed by Google's Privacy Policy, not ours.
  • Anonymised analytics: Firebase Analytics data is anonymised and cannot be linked to any individual after account deletion. This aggregate data may be retained for statistical purposes.
  • Legal records: If required by law (e.g., financial records, legal disputes), we may be required to retain certain data for the legally mandated retention period.
