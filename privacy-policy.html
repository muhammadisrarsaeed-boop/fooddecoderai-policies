🤖 6. AI & Automated Processing

AI-Generated Content Disclaimer: All recipe content, nutritional information, ingredient lists, and cooking instructions provided by Food Decoder AI are generated by artificial intelligence (Google Gemini AI). This content is for informational and entertainment purposes only.

How AI Processing Works

  1. You photograph or describe a food item
  2. Our app uses Google ML Kit on your device to detect general food labels (no data leaves device at this step)
  3. The image or description is sent to Google Gemini AI via a secure HTTPS API call
  4. Gemini AI returns a structured recipe including ingredients, steps, and nutritional estimates
  5. The result is displayed in the app and saved to your history

Important AI Limitations

  • AI-generated recipes may contain errors, inaccuracies, or may not reflect authentic culinary traditions
  • Nutritional information is estimated and should not be used for medical, dietary, or clinical purposes
  • Allergen warnings are AI-estimated and not a substitute for reading actual product labels
  • AI food recognition may misidentify dishes, especially complex or regional foods

No Automated Decision-Making

We do not use your data for any automated decision-making that produces legal or similarly significant effects on you. AI processing is used solely to generate recipe content from your food images.

🔐 7. Data Storage & Security

Where Data is Stored

  • Firebase (Google Cloud): Your account profile, saved recipes, favourites, and scan history are stored on Google's Firebase infrastructure, which uses industry-standard security measures.
  • Your Device: Preferences, shopping lists, meal plans, and fridge/pantry data are stored locally in an encrypted database on your device.
  • Not Stored: Food photos you scan are processed in real-time and are not stored on our servers.

Security Measures

  • All data in transit is encrypted using TLS/HTTPS — we enforce HTTPS for all network communications and do not permit cleartext traffic
  • Firebase Authentication uses industry-standard OAuth 2.0 and secure token handling
  • Firestore security rules restrict data access so users can only read and write their own data
  • We do not store passwords — authentication is handled entirely by Firebase Auth or Google Sign-In
  • App binaries are signed with a private release key for integrity verification
Data Backup: Local device data (shopping lists, meal plans) is excluded from Android cloud backup to prevent unintentional data exposure. Your cloud data (recipes, favourites) is securely synced via Firebase Firestore.
